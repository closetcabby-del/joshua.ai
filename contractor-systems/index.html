FAQ

Questions contractors ask before starting.

Do I own the website and my business accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website assets, analytics, CRM data, files and core business accounts stay under your control.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Ongoing support is optional. You can self-manage after handoff or bring me back when you need help.

Can you connect the tools I already use?

Usually. QuickBooks, Google Workspace, CRMs and other tools can often be connected. I’ll confirm what is technically possible before an integration is included in scope.

Do you put AI into every project?

No. AI is only used when it makes the business easier to run. If a website, lead process or SOP system solves the problem, that’s what we’ll build.
