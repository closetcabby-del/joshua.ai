FAQ

Questions contractors ask before starting.

Do I own the website and my business accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website assets, analytics, CRM data, files and core business accounts stay under your control.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Care plans are optional. You can self-manage after handoff or bring me back when you need help.

Can you improve my current website instead of replacing it?

Yes, if the current platform is worth keeping. I’ll tell you whether improving it or rebuilding it makes more sense.

Do you write the website content?

Yes. Core messaging, page structure and conversion copy are included within scope. Large content projects are quoted separately.

How many revisions are included?

Two structured revision rounds are included. New pages, new functionality or major direction changes are additional scope.

What happens after launch?

I handle final testing, handoff and agreed-scope fixes during a short post-launch period. After that, you can self-manage or choose ongoing support.

Can you connect QuickBooks, Google Workspace or a CRM?

Usually. QuickBooks, Google Workspace, CRMs and other tools can often be connected. I’ll confirm what is technically possible before including an integration in your scope.

Do you build AI into every project?

No. AI is only used when it makes the business easier to run. If a website, lead process or SOP system solves the problem, that’s what we’ll build.
