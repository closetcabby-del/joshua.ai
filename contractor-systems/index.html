FAQ

Questions a contractor should not have to chase you for.

Do I own the website and my business accounts?

Yes. The intended model is client ownership of the domain, website assets, analytics, CRM data, business files and core accounts. Ongoing support is optional.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Monthly care plans are optional. Project packages are scoped as standalone implementations with handoff. Recurring care is available when you want ongoing improvements or support.

Can you improve my current website instead of replacing it?

Yes, when the current platform and code are workable. The first step is an audit to determine whether improvement is more economical than rebuilding.

Do you write the website content?

Basic messaging, page structure and conversion copy are included within the agreed package scope. Large-scale copywriting, extensive SEO content or large page inventories are separately scoped.

How many revisions are included?

Two structured revision rounds are included. Each round should be one consolidated list of feedback. New pages, new functionality or a major direction change are treated as additional scope.

What happens after launch?

Launch includes QA, handoff and a short stabilization period for agreed-scope fixes. After that, you can maintain the system yourself, request work as needed, or choose a monthly care plan.

Can you connect this to QuickBooks, Google Workspace or a CRM?

Yes, when the selected tools expose the required integration path and permissions. Custom integrations are scoped separately because complexity varies significantly between systems.

Do you build AI into every project?

No. AI is used only where it creates a practical advantage. A contractor that primarily needs a better website, lead process or SOP structure should not be forced into an AI project.
