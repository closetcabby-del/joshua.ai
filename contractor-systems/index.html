FAQ

Questions a contractor should not have to chase you for.

Do I own the website and my business accounts?

Yes. The intended model is client ownership of the domain, website assets, analytics, CRM data, business files and core accounts. Ongoing support is optional.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Monthly care plans are optional. Project packages are standalone implementations with handoff.

Can you improve my current website instead of replacing it?

Yes, when the current platform and code are workable. The first step is determining whether improvement is more economical than rebuilding.

Do you write the website content?

Basic messaging, page structure and conversion copy are included within the agreed scope. Large content inventories are separately scoped.

How many revisions are included?

Two structured revision rounds. Each round should be one consolidated list of feedback. New pages, new functionality or major direction changes are additional scope.

What happens after launch?

Launch includes QA, handoff and a short stabilization period for agreed-scope fixes. Then you can self-manage, request work as needed or choose a care plan.

Can you connect QuickBooks, Google Workspace or a CRM?

Yes when the tools expose the required integration path and permissions. Custom integrations are scoped separately because complexity varies.

Do you build AI into every project?

No. AI is used only where it creates a practical advantage. A contractor that mainly needs a website, lead process or SOP structure should not be forced into an AI project.
