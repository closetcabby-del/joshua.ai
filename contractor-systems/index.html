How projects work

A controlled process protects the client and the project.

Client meetings, feedback, revisions, QA and launch are already part of the delivery model.

01

Discover

Goals, services, customers, current systems and content.

02

Design + Build

Create the first working version against agreed scope.

03

Client Review

Present the system and collect consolidated feedback.

04

Revise + Test

Two structured revision rounds plus responsive QA/UAT.

05

Launch

Deployment, handoff and a defined stabilization period.

Revision boundary

Two structured rounds

One consolidated feedback list per round. New functionality is separately scoped.

Delivery principle

Total client time counts

Kickoff, presentations, feedback, revisions, testing and launch are treated as real delivery work.

Scope changes

Protect the outcome

Major direction changes, added pages or additional workflows are quoted before work begins.

Ongoing support

Keep the system healthy after launch.

Optional monthly plans create a clear support boundary while giving clients a long-term technology partner.
