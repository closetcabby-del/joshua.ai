# Clean and practical app direction

Selected by the user: clean and practical, like a polished app. Charcoal and monochrome are retained.

Manrope is self-hosted at weights 400, 500, 600, and 700. All prominent typography is sans-serif in normal style. Smaller headlines, readable body text, straightforward navigation, clear starting choices, and softly rounded controls replace the dramatic editorial treatment.

The brand uses a simple J badge. Decorative AI icons, orbit artwork, sparkles, serif lettering, and italic headings are absent. Standard navigation, playback, copy, and privacy controls remain.

The 15-second HyperFrames introduction uses matching Manrope typography and the same J badge. It retains captions, manual playback, and a static poster.

The implementation preserves all candidate facts, evidence labels, and privacy behavior. Live AI remains in demonstration mode until actual approved evidence and external configuration exist.
